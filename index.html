About the game

أقوى صورة (Aqwa Soura) is a meme party game for 3 to 8 players. Each round, one player reads a meme sentence out loud, like "when you open the front camera by accident". Every other player secretly picks the funniest image for it from the 6 images in their hand. The sentence owner then sees all the picks shuffled, without names, and chooses the winner. The best meme gets the point.

لعبة ميمز جماعية من ٣ إلى ٨ لاعبين. في كل جولة يقرأ لاعب جملة ميم بصوت عالٍ، ويختار كل لاعب آخر أقوى صورة تناسبها من بين ٦ صور في يده. صاحب الجملة يختار الصورة الفائزة دون أن يعرف من اختارها. العبوا على جوال واحد يدور بينكم، أو كلٌّ على جواله عبر نفس شبكة الواي فاي.

Two ways to play

One device · جهاز واحد

Add everyone's name and pass a single phone from player to player. The game tells you whose turn it is.

Local Wi-Fi · شبكة واي فاي محلية

One player opens a room and shares its 5-digit code. Everyone else joins with the code on their own phone. All phones must be on the same Wi-Fi.

How to play

  1. Choose how you want to play, add 3 to 8 players, and start the game.
  2. Each round, one player is the sentence owner and reads the meme sentence out loud to everyone.
  3. Everyone else looks at their 6 images, keeping their screen hidden, and picks the one that fits the sentence best.
  4. The sentence owner sees all the picks shuffled and unlabelled and chooses the funniest. Whoever picked it scores a point.
  5. The sentence passes to the next player every round. Whoever has the most points at the end is the champion.

Common questions

How many players do we need?

At least 3 and up to 8, on one phone or on several phones over Wi-Fi.

Does the game need an internet connection?

You don't need internet to play. One-device mode works fully offline. In Wi-Fi mode, the phones connect to each other directly over your local network, so the Wi-Fi only has to be shared, not connected to the internet. An internet connection is only needed to load ads and to make or restore purchases.

My friends can't find my room ("No room with that code on this network").

  • Make sure every phone is on the same Wi-Fi network. A phone using mobile data can't see the room.
  • Check that the code has all 5 digits and that the host still has the room open.
  • Some public, hotel, office or university networks, and many "guest" networks, stop devices from seeing each other. If that happens, try a home Wi-Fi network.
  • On iPhone, the game needs the Local Network permission. If you declined it, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

The room refused to let me join.

The room is either full (8 players) or the game has already started. Ask the host to finish the current game, or to open a new room.

Someone lost their connection during a game.

Tap Rejoin (ارجع للغرفة) while connected to the same Wi-Fi. If the host left the app, the host needs to tap Reopen the room (شغّل الغرفة) so the others can rejoin.

Will we see the same images or sentences again?

Not in the same game until you've been through all of them. Every image and every sentence in your selected packs is used once before any of them comes back, even when you play with both the Free and Premium packs together.

I bought Premium but the content is still locked.

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases (استعادة الشراء). This checks your purchase with the App Store or Google Play again and unlocks it. Do the same after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Do I need to pay again on a new phone?

No. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID or Google account. Install the game, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Are our scores saved?

No. Player names and scores only last while the game is open. They are cleared when you close the app. Nothing is uploaded or kept on our side.

An image or sentence is offensive, or I own an image and want it removed.

Please email us with a screenshot or a short description of the image or sentence. We review every report and remove content that shouldn't be in the game.

I found a bug.

Email us with what happened, which mode you were playing (one device or Wi-Fi), and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot. Bug reports are really useful and we fix them quickly.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information أقوى صورة ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login or sign-up of any kind. We do not run servers for the game, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personally identifying information through normal use of the App.

Player Names and Game Data

To play, you type a name for each player. Names are used only to show whose turn it is and to keep score during the game:

  • Names and scores are kept in your device's memory only while the game is running. They are not saved after you close the App.
  • In Local Wi-Fi mode, your player name, the room code and your in-game actions (such as the image you pick and the scores) are sent directly to the other phones in the same room over your local network. They are never sent to us or to any server we operate.
  • Other players in your room can see the name you enter. You are free to use a nickname.

Local Network Access

In Local Wi-Fi mode, the App uses your Wi-Fi network to find the room that matches the code you enter and to exchange game data between the phones in that room. This traffic stays on your local network. On iPhone, the system will ask for permission to access your Local Network. The permission is only needed for Wi-Fi mode, and one-device mode works without it.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App may save a small amount of information locally on your own device, such as whether you own a Premium purchase so that the content stays unlocked. This information is never sent to us and is removed if you uninstall the App.

We do not control this data collection directly. It is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review: policies.google.com/privacy. You can also read how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown. On iPhone, if the App asks for permission to track your activity, you can decline. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

You can limit ad personalization at any time from your device's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on iOS).

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases, such as Premium, which unlocks extra content like additional meme images and sentences. The free version of the game is fully playable without buying anything.

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase was completed, so the App can unlock what you bought. Restoring a previous purchase asks the store whether your account already owns it, and no personal data reaches us in the process.

Data Security

The App has no accounts and no game servers, so there is no central database of user data on our side that could be exposed in a breach. Game data only travels between the phones in your room on your local network, and data handled by Google AdMob and the app stores is protected under their own security practices.
