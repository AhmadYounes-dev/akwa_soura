About the game

أقوى صورة (Aqwa Soura) is a meme party game for 3 to 10 players. Each round, one player reads a meme sentence out loud, like "when you open the front camera by accident". Every other player secretly picks the funniest image for it from the 6 images in their hand. The sentence owner then sees all the picks shuffled, without names, and chooses the winner. The best meme gets the point.

لعبة جماعية من ٣ إلى ١٠ لاعبين. في كل جولة يقرأ أحد اللاعبين جملة مضحكة بصوت عالٍ، ويختار كل لاعب آخر من بين ٦ صور مضحكة في يده الصورة التي تناسبها أكثر. ثم يختار صاحب الجملة الصورة الفائزة دون أن يعرف من اختارها. العبوا على هاتف واحد تتناقلونه بينكم، أو يلعب كلٌّ على هاتفه عبر الإنترنت، بالواي فاي أو ببيانات الهاتف، أينما كنتم.

Two ways to play

On one device · على جهاز واحد

Add everyone's name and pass a single phone from player to player. The game tells you whose turn it is. This mode works fully offline.

Over Wi-Fi · عبر الواي فاي

Everyone plays on their own phone, over Wi-Fi or mobile data, from anywhere. One player creates a room and shares its 8-digit code. The others tap Join game (انضم إلى لعبة) and enter the code. Phones don't need to be on the same network or in the same place.

In an online room, the player who opened it runs it: they choose the content, start the game and move everyone on to each new turn.

How to play

  1. Choose how you want to play: add 3 to 10 players on one phone, or open a room and have your friends join it. Choose a save file, then start the game.
  2. Each round, one player is the sentence owner and reads the meme sentence out loud to everyone.
  3. Everyone else looks at their 6 images, keeping their screen hidden, and picks the one that fits the sentence best.
  4. The sentence owner sees all the picks shuffled and unlabelled and chooses the funniest. Whoever picked it scores a point.
  5. The sentence passes to the next player every round. Whoever has the most points at the end is the champion.

Save files

Each mode has its own save files (up to 20). A save file remembers which images and sentences have already come up, so nothing repeats, even across games, until everything has been used or you reset it with Reset (تصفير) on the setup screen. Choose a save file before you start a game. In an online room, the game uses the save file of the player who opened the room.

You can copy a save file to the other mode, or send it to a friend: your friend taps Receive a file (استقبال ملف) and gives you the 6-digit code that appears, and you enter it after tapping Send a file (إرسال ملف). The file arrives in the same mode it was sent from.

Common questions

How many players do we need?

At least 3 and up to 10, either on one phone or each on their own phone online.

Does the game need an internet connection?

One-device mode works fully offline. Playing on separate phones (Over Wi-Fi) needs an internet connection on every phone. Wi-Fi and mobile data both work, and you don't need to be on the same network. You also need internet to send or receive a save file from a friend, to load ads, and to buy or restore Premium.

My friends can't join my room ("No room with that code").

  • Check that they entered all 8 digits of the room code.
  • Make sure every phone is connected to the internet.
  • Make sure the room is still open. A room closes automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left it. If it closed, open a new one.
  • Make sure everyone has the latest version of the game. The game asks you to update when a newer version is needed.

The room refused to let me join.

The room is either full (10 players) or its game has already started. A player who was already in that game can always come back to their seat from the same phone (see below). Otherwise, ask the player running the room to finish the current game, or to open a new room.

Someone lost their connection or left during a game.

On the same phone, they tap Join game (انضم إلى لعبة) and enter the same room code again. They get their seat back with their score. If the player running the room leaves, the player who has been in the room longest takes over.

Will we see the same images or sentences again?

Not until you've been through all of them. The save file you play on remembers every image and sentence that has come up, even across games. They only come back once everything in your selected packs has been used, or when you reset them yourself. This also works when you play with both the Free and Premium packs together.

Does everyone in an online room need Premium?

No. One player in the room owning Premium is enough for the whole room to play with the Premium content.

I bought Premium but the content is still locked.

Make sure you are connected to the internet, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases (استعادة الشراء). This checks your purchase with the App Store or Google Play again and unlocks it. Do the same after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Do I need to pay again on a new phone?

No. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID or Google account. Install the game, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Save files stay on the old phone, so to keep your progress, send them to the new phone with Send a file → To a friend.

Are our scores saved?

No. Player names and scores only last while a game is running. A save file keeps which images and sentences have come up, not scores. Save files stay on your phone. Online rooms are deleted from our server automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left.

An image or sentence is offensive, or I own an image and want it removed.

Please email us with a screenshot or a short description of the image or sentence. We review every report and remove content that shouldn't be in the game.

I found a bug.

Email us with what happened, which mode you were playing (one device or Over Wi-Fi), and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot. Bug reports are really useful and we fix them quickly.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information أقوى صورة ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login or sign-up of any kind. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the App does not access them.

Player Names and Game Data

To play, you type a name for each player. Names are used only to show whose turn it is and to keep score:

  • In one-device mode, names and scores stay on your phone, only while the game is running. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • In Over Wi-Fi mode, the App sends the following to our game server so the room can run: the name you enter, the room code, and your in-game actions (such as the image you pick, the winner chosen and the scores). For the player who opens the room, it also sends the name of their selected save file and its progress (which images and sentences have already come up). The server shares names, picks and scores with the other players in the same room, as the game requires.
  • Room data is kept on the server only while the room is in use, and is deleted automatically about 20 minutes after everyone has left the room.
  • Other players in your room can see the name you enter. You are free to use a nickname.

Save Files

Save files are stored on your device. A save file contains the name you gave it and which images and sentences have already come up. It contains no personal information. Copying a save file to the other mode happens entirely on your device.

When you send a save file to a friend, the file is uploaded to our server and held only until your friend's phone receives it. It is deleted automatically, at the latest 10 minutes after your friend's code was created.

Our Game Server

Online rooms and save file transfers run on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. All connections to it are encrypted. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to deliver the connection, and keeps short technical logs (such as the time of a request and basic connection details) for a few days, which we use only to keep the service working and fix problems. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not sell or share it.

Google Firebase

The App uses Google Firebase for two things:

  • Update check: when the App starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version of the App is required. Firebase uses a random installation identifier and basic information (such as the App version and platform) for this.
  • Purchase check: when you buy or restore Premium, the purchase receipt issued by the store is checked by our service on Firebase, as described under In-App Purchases below.

Firebase is operated by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your own device:

  • Your save files, and which one is selected in each mode.
  • The name you used for online play, and the code of the last room you were in, so you can get back into it quickly.
  • Whether you own Premium, so the content stays unlocked.
  • Your ad consent choices, where they apply.

Save files only leave your device if you send one to a friend. The rest of this information is never sent to us. All of it is removed if you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App shows ads served through Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads between rounds, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus (for example, swapping an image in your hand). Buying Premium removes all ads.

To serve and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, basic device information, and how you interact with ads. We do not control this data collection directly. It is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review: policies.google.com/privacy. You can also read how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown. On iPhone, if the App asks for permission to track your activity, you can decline. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

You can limit ad personalization at any time from your device's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on iOS).

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase, Premium, which unlocks extra meme images and sentences and removes all ads. The free version of the game is fully playable without buying anything.

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). To make sure a purchase is genuine, the App sends the purchase receipt issued by the store to our purchase-check service (Google Cloud Functions, through Firebase), which asks Google Play or Apple to confirm it. The receipt contains no payment details. We only keep a short log of which product was checked and whether it was confirmed. Restoring a purchase works the same way.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information (for example, through the contact email below), please contact us and we will remove it.

Links to Other Apps and Sites

If You Contact Us

If you email us for support, to report a bug or to report content, we will receive whatever information you choose to include in that email (such as your email address and message), and we use it only to respond to you.

Data Security

The App has no accounts and we keep no database of users. Game data travels over encrypted connections. Online rooms and save files sent to friends are deleted automatically after the short times described above. Data handled by Google (AdMob and Firebase), Cloudflare and the app stores is protected under their own security practices.

Your Choices

  • You can use a nickname instead of your real name.
  • You can limit ad personalization or withdraw ad consent from your device's settings, as described above.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data described above.
  • You can contact us at any time with questions or requests about your data.
